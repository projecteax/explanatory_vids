# Moon explainer — directed opening (0:00–0:30)

The lab is the physical stage throughout. Educational graphics live on Bravo's
monitor and expand out of it only when Bravo starts explaining.

## Shot plan

1. **0:00–0:02.5 — Establishing push-in**
   Wide lab geography. Pipi flies in from frame-right, rotors streaking. The
   center monitor shows a full Moon. A red beacon and "Moon emergency" establish
   Pipi's worried interpretation immediately.
2. **0:02.5–0:06.3 — Evidence insert**
   Camera pushes toward Pipi and the workbench. Full Moon vs crescent evidence
   pops above the lab while the physical monitor switches to alert red.
3. **0:06.3–0:10.9 — Pipi close-up**
   Hard comedic close-up with a slight handheld alarm shake. Pipi fills frame;
   the lab remains visible behind her. The accusation lands as a visual punchline.
4. **0:10.9–0:11.65 — Reaction pause**
   Cut wide enough to reveal Bravo. Silence gets its own beat before his answer.
5. **0:11.65–0:16.7 — Bravo reassurance**
   Medium close-up. Bravo's supplied vector art is rigged into a relaxed pose;
   he gestures while speaking. Slow dolly-in emphasizes calm confidence.
6. **0:16.7–0:20.6 — Screen-motivated move**
   Camera leaves Bravo and pushes toward the crescent monitor as he says it only
   *looks* different. Pipi follows the viewer's attention.
7. **0:20.6–0:24.9 — Comedy energy beat**
   Pipi close-up, faster bob and rotor accents. "SCIENCE SNACK!" enters as a
   dimensional sticker with confetti particles.
8. **0:24.9–0:30 — Lesson begins**
   Back in a lab wide, the central monitor grows into the educational stage.
   Bravo points. A dimensional Moon ball rotates while a line draws on and labels
   it. This continues naturally into the rest of the explanation.

## Direction rules

- Camera changes are motivated by the active speaker or the object being taught.
- No generic title-card layout; the lab remains spatially coherent.
- Supplied Bravo, Pipi, and lab references are traced to SVG without repainting.
- Bravo's T-pose is split into clipped vector layers and rigged at the shoulders.
- Captions use approximate word-progress highlighting and preserve the full VO.
- Cyan is science/information; orange is comedy; red is Pipi's false alarm.
